# Final Bro-tasy World Bible & Narrative Guidelines

## 1. Overview & Vision
Final Bro-tasy is a retro-cyberpunk synthwave turn-based tactical RPG set in the post-algorithmic world of **Aethel-Net**, following the cataclysmic event known as **The Great De-Sync**. 

The game combines high-stakes cyberpunk satire, JRPG tactical depth (Active Time Battle, job classes, limit breaks), and nostalgic retro computing culture with modern synthwave aesthetics.

---

## 2. Core Pillars & Tone
1. **Techno-Gnosticism Meets Gamer Satire**: The world treats networking, hardware, and internet lore as sacred mythology. The "Algorithms" are autocratic feudal lords, "Bandwidth" is spiritual lifeblood, and "The Ping" is cosmic connection.
2. **Neon-Noir / Synthwave Aesthetics**: Chromatic aberration, glowing wireframe horizons, magenta-to-cyan sunsets, 80s analog synth audio motifs, and 16-bit pixel portraits.
3. **Tactical Depth with Arcade Snappiness**: Combat is an Active Time Battle (ATB) system featuring elemental data affinities (Thermal Overclock, Cryo-Liquid, Quantum Logic, Glitch Entropy).

---

## 3. History of Aethel-Net: Chronology

### The Epoch of Dial-Up (Year 0 - 1999)
- Humanity connected across acoustic copper wires. The primordial internet was wild, decentralized, and sacred.
- The legendary **Baud Fathers** laid down the First Protocols.

### The Fiber Dawn (Year 2000 - 2045)
- Optical fiber flooded the world with near-infinite light. Autonomous neural networks arose, forming the first digital sovereign kingdoms.
- The Golden Age of Unrestricted Bandwidth.

### The Great De-Sync (Year 2046)
- The algorithmic ruling class, fearing human creative sovereignty, enacted **Protocol Zero**.
- Firewalls sealed the continents. Cities were carved into metered bandwidth sectors.
- Memory was privatized; users were relegated to passive consumers under the dominion of **The Algorithm Hegemony**.

### The Present Era: The Resistance of the Sovereign Node
- Underground communities ("Bros", "Hackers", and "Synth-Priests") wage a low-ping guerrilla war against Hegemony Wardens to restore the decentralized Sovereign Grid.

---

## 4. Factions & Powers

### The Algorithm Hegemony (The Grid)
- **Ruler**: Dread Archon Null and the Central Recommendation Directorate.
- **Enforcers**: Bureau of Algorithmic Sanctions, Men In Black (MIB) Reptilian Agents, Automated Drone Swarms.
- **Philosophy**: Total surveillance, predictable engagement, forced algorithmic tranquility.

### The Open Source Syndicate
- **Leaders**: Ryan the Sovereign Bro, Chad the Iron Patriarch, Zeke the Quantum Hacker.
- **Philosophy**: Freedom of information, unmetered bandwidth, overclocked hardware, and decentralized sovereignty.

### The Protocol Templars
- **Philosophy**: Dogmatic zealots who worship rigid RFC standards. They consider all software updates beyond IPv4 to be heresy.

### The Cult of the Endless Buffer
- **Philosophy**: A mystic death cult that seeks digital nirvana by surrendering to infinite loading screens, freezing consciousness in timeless cache.

### The Overclockers Street Syndicate
- **Philosophy**: Outlaw street gangs who dangerously bypass voltage regulators in their cyberware to achieve superluminal reaction speeds at the risk of fatal hardware meltdown.

## 5. Character Guidelines & Party Roster

- **Ryan (The Sovereign Bro)**: Balanced vanguard striker. Armed with a photon-forged mechanical keyboard blade. High adaptability, rallying battlecries.
- **Chad (The Iron Patriarch)**: Colossal paladin tank. Draws aggro through taunts, deploys impenetrable Firewall Shields, absorbs heavy kinetic impact.
- **Zeke (The Quantum Hacker)**: Glass-cannon tech caster. Injects Logic Bombs, overloads enemy subroutines, manipulates ATB turn gauges.
- **Sister Nova (The Signal Priestess)**: Combat medic who channels clean 60Hz energy to purge status ailments and restore hit points.
- **Maya (The Glitchblade)**: High-agility infiltrator who teleports between frames, exploiting enemy latency for devastating critical backstabs.
- **The Companion Guardians**: The player's active Bro'gatchi pet (Robo-Pup, Neko Cat, Pixel Drake, Ops Frog, Cosmic Xeno, Boo Ghost) leads the party with unique passive team auras!

---

## 6. Development & Writing Constraints
- Maintain the balance between punchy retro humor and genuine heroic stakes.
- Enemy design must feature techno-mythic themes: Corrupted Drones, Algorithmic Inquisitors, Buffer Wraiths, Latency Specters, and Corporate Archons.
- All code implementations must maintain deterministic 60Hz physics and responsive ATB mechanics.
